# Helios website brief (heliosgroup.ai)

Use this when the lead wants more information about Helios and the positioning document does not clearly answer. Prefer referring them to the site over inventing detail.

## What to tell the lead

- Visible text in the email must be exactly: `heliosgroup.ai`
- That text is hyperlinked to `https://heliosgroup.ai` by the send path.
- Do not use "https://heliosgroup.ai" as the visible anchor text.
- Do not say "click here" or "our website" without also writing `heliosgroup.ai`.

## What the site covers

- What Helios does: forward-deployed AI engineering for agentic marketing and sales systems, implementation, integration, and shipping (not slideware-only consulting).
- Who we are: builders who integrate systems, train teams to run them, and build custom tools when off-the-shelf falls short.
- Case studies / examples of work: present on the site. Do not invent case-study details in email. If they ask for examples, point them to `heliosgroup.ai`.

## When to use this referral

- Questions about services, approach, industries, team, or proof that are not clearly answered in the positioning document.
- Requests for "more info", "deck", "examples", "what do you do", or similar.
- Default when in doubt: refer to the site, then nudge Calendly for the real conversation.
