
## Who Embark is

Embark is a financial consulting and advisory firm focused on optimizing finance, technology, HR, and operations. Embark is not a CPA firm and does not perform audits or tax services; Embark supports companies in preparing for audits and ensuring financial statements are accurate and compliant.

Embark positions around strategic finance and business transformation with hands-on execution. The team is composed of Big 4 alumni and seasoned industry leaders. Client satisfaction / NPS is over 70% higher than the industry average.

Embark operates with a nationwide footprint and supports hybrid and on-site engagement models.

## Outcomes we help clients achieve

- Optimize financial operations
- Modernize tech and data
